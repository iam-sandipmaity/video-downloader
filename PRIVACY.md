# Privacy Policy

## Summary

Video Downloader is designed as a local-first Android app.

That means the app's normal behavior is:

- links are analyzed on your device
- downloads are executed on your device
- conversion and compression run on your device
- saved files are written to your device storage
- the app does not require its own backend account system

## What The App Does Process

Depending on the feature you use, the app may process:

- links you paste or share into the app
- download format choices and filename settings
- cookies you explicitly save for supported sites
- YouTube access/session recovery data you explicitly generate in-app
- local media files you choose for conversion or compression
- app logs stored on-device for troubleshooting

## What The App Does Not Intend To Do

The app is not designed to:

- upload your downloads to a project-owned cloud
- require a central project account
- track you with an app-owned analytics backend
- move your saved media through a project server for processing

## Third-Party Network Requests

The app may contact third-party services when you ask it to do so, including:

- media websites you paste into the app
- GitHub release endpoints for app, `yt-dlp`, or FFmpeg update checks
- upstream media delivery/CDN endpoints used during downloads

Those requests are part of the downloader and update flows themselves.

## Cookies And YouTube Access

If you choose to use Cookies or YouTube access features:

- cookie/session data is stored on-device
- the app uses that data to make authenticated download requests on your behalf
- you control when cookie/session data is added, updated, exported, or deleted

Do not import or share cookie files unless you understand what account/session
access they contain.

## Logs

The app can keep local logs for queue diagnostics, runtime output, and
troubleshooting.

Current behavior:

- logs stay on-device unless you explicitly copy or export them
- exported logs may include URLs, runtime messages, file paths, and error text
- you should review logs before sharing them publicly

## File Storage

Downloaded media and exported logs are stored on your device, usually under the
public downloads area configured in the app.

The app may also use app-owned internal or external storage for temporary files,
runtime packages, and cached data needed for normal operation.

## User Responsibility

You are responsible for:

- the links you download
- whether you store authenticated cookies
- whether you share logs, screenshots, or downloaded media
- complying with the terms, copyright rules, and laws that apply in your region

## Changes To This Policy

If the app later adds a behavior that materially changes privacy expectations,
this file should be updated together with the release documentation.
