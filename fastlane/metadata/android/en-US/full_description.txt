Video Downloader is a local-first Android app for downloading and managing media directly on your device.

It uses yt-dlp for extraction and FFmpeg for post-processing, with no required backend, no cloud conversion service, and no forced account system.

Core features:
- single video, audio-only, and playlist downloads
- per-item format, naming, and container choices
- queue controls with pause, resume, retry, and diagnostics
- local downloads library with built-in audio and video playback
- cookies and YouTube access helpers for tougher sites
- on-device conversion and compression tools

The app is built for real-world recovery instead of one-tap magic. You can inspect history, retry failed tasks, manage saved files, and handle post-processing without leaving the app.

Everything runs on-device, so link handling, downloads, merging, and follow-up media processing stay local to your Android device.
